# Affiliate Disclosure

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

BudgetBrewLab is a member of the **eBay Partner Network**, an affiliate program: when you click one of our eBay links and buy something, eBay may pay us a commission.

Our Amazon links are not yet part of the Amazon Associates program. Until our membership is approved they are ordinary links and earn us nothing.

## What this means in plain English

- Some links on this site point to products on eBay or Amazon. The eBay links are affiliate links today; the Amazon links become affiliate links only once Amazon approves our membership.
- If you click an affiliate link and buy something, the retailer pays us a small percentage of the sale.
- **You pay exactly the same price** whether you use our link or not. The commission comes out of the retailer's side, not yours.

## What it does NOT mean

- We are not paid by manufacturers to recommend their products.
- Nobody sends us free gear in exchange for coverage.
- Our commission is the same small percentage whether you buy the $30 pick or the $150 pick, so we have no incentive to upsell you — and you'll notice we often recommend the cheaper option.

## Accuracy

Prices quoted in articles are typical street prices at the time each article was written. Coffee gear goes on sale constantly, and prices you see on Amazon may be higher or lower. Always confirm the current price, seller, and product version on the retailer's page before purchasing. Product specifications can also change between revisions; when in doubt, verify on the manufacturer's site.

## Why we disclose this

The FTC requires that sites disclose material connections between themselves and the products they link to, and Amazon's Associates Program Operating Agreement requires a clear statement on every page that contains affiliate links. That's why you see the short disclosure line at the top of every page on this site.
